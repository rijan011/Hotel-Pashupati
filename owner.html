Surendra Bhattarai is a Nepalese entrepreneur, hotel owner, and hospitality businessman who has built a diversified portfolio of businesses through dedication, perseverance, and a long-term vision. Best known as the founder of Hotel Pashupati in Chandrapur, Rautahat, Surendra Bhattarai has gradually expanded his entrepreneurial journey into hotels, restaurants, banquet services, travel and tourism, and retail.

Born on 4 April 1979 in Bayarban-07, Morang, Nepal, Surendra Bhattarai grew up with the belief that success comes through consistent hard work, discipline, and integrity. After completing his Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS), he chose entrepreneurship over conventional employment, determined to create opportunities not only for himself but also for his community.

Today, Surendra Bhattarai is recognised by customers and business associates for his commitment to quality hospitality, customer satisfaction, and business development. His ventures contribute to local employment and support tourism and hospitality in Nepal.

Early Life and Education

Surendra Bhattarai was born in Bayarban-07 of Morang District. Raised in a family that valued honesty, responsibility, and perseverance, he learned from an early age that meaningful achievements are earned through dedication and continuous effort.

His academic journey led him to pursue a Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS), where he gained a solid understanding of business management, finance, marketing, and entrepreneurship. While his education provided a theoretical foundation, his greatest lessons came from practical experience and years of hands-on business management.

Beginning an Entrepreneurial Journey

Like many entrepreneurs, Surendra Bhattarai’s journey was not defined by instant success. Building a business from the ground up required patience, calculated decision-making, and the willingness to overcome challenges.

Starting with limited resources, he focused on earning the trust of customers by delivering reliable service and maintaining high standards. Rather than pursuing rapid expansion, he adopted a gradual growth strategy, reinvesting in his businesses and strengthening operations over time.

This disciplined approach helped lay the foundation for what would become a diversified business portfolio.

Establishing Hotel Pashupati

A significant milestone came on Ashar 15, 2065 B.S., with the establishment of Hotel Pashupati in Chandrapur, Rautahat.

Surendra Bhattarai — Founder & Managing Director

Strategically located along the Mahendra Highway, Hotel Pashupati was founded with the vision of providing comfortable accommodation, quality dining, and dependable hospitality for business travellers, families, tourists, and visitors to the region. Over the years, the hotel has evolved into a recognised hospitality destination in Chandrapur, offering modern rooms, event facilities, and authentic Nepali hospitality. The success of Hotel Pashupati became the cornerstone of Surendra Bhattarai’s broader entrepreneurial journey, creating opportunities for further investment in hospitality and related industries.

Building a Diversified Business Portfolio

Driven by a vision for sustainable growth, Surendra Bhattarai expanded beyond a single hotel into multiple businesses serving different customer needs. His business portfolio includes:

  • Hotel Pashupati (Flagship Hotel Destination)
  • Hotel Aarohi (Luxury Accommodation)
  • Golden Banquet (Event & Wedding Venue)
  • Family Restaurant (Dining Experience)
  • Nepali Bhansa Ghar (Authentic Nepali Cuisine)
  • Yasoda Travels & Tours (Tourism & Travel Services)
  • Extra Variety Department Store (Retail Business)

Together, these businesses reflect a commitment to providing quality hospitality, dining, travel, and retail services while supporting local economic activity.

Leadership Philosophy

Surendra Bhattarai believes that successful businesses are built on trust, consistency, and service excellence. His leadership philosophy centres on:

  • Putting customers first
  • Investing in long-term relationships
  • Maintaining ethical business practices
  • Creating employment opportunities
  • Continuously improving service quality
  • Supporting local communities

This approach has guided the steady growth of his enterprises while helping establish a reputation for reliability.

Commitment to Community

Beyond business, Surendra Bhattarai has participated in community-support initiatives during times of need.

During the COVID-19 pandemic, he supported local relief efforts by assisting economically vulnerable individuals and families. Following the 2072 Nepal Earthquake, he also contributed to relief activities and initiatives that helped local communities recover and restore normal market operations.

He is also associated with the Hotel Entrepreneurs Association of Rautahat, reflecting his engagement with Nepal’s hospitality sector and local business community.

Looking Ahead

As Nepal’s hospitality industry continues to evolve, Surendra Bhattarai remains focused on strengthening his businesses while improving guest experiences and contributing to regional tourism.

His vision is to continue expanding responsibly, embrace innovation, and promote hospitality that reflects Nepal’s culture, warmth, and professionalism.

With a growing portfolio of hospitality and service-oriented businesses, Surendra Bhattarai continues to work toward creating lasting value for customers, employees, business partners, and the wider community.
